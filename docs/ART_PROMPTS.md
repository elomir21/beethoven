Input image is the EXACT identity and existing animation poses to preserve: Beethoven is the distinctive white-and-caramel small terrier with asymmetrical ears (one tall upright ear, one folded ear), white muzzle, dark nose, long tail, and caramel patches. Reorganize and carefully clean this reference into an EXACTLY 4 COLUMN by 3 ROW sprite atlas, 12 equal cells, ideally 2048x1152 total (512x384 cells). Preserve this same dog's identity, markings, palette, silhouette, pixel-art-inspired illustration style and twelve existing poses, with clearer edges and refined fur detail. Row1 standing idle then three walk poses; row2 four running stride poses; row3 rising jump, midair jump, landing crouch, happy alert. All poses side profile FACING RIGHT. Same character size in all cells, all feet aligned at 88 percent cell height (jump poses also individually baseline-aligned for engine animation), visible entire body with transparent padding, character fills 80 percent cell width and 72 percent cell height. Remove ALL red guides, bright red fringes, halos and stray pixels. Transparent background, no ground, no shadows, no grid, no words, no labels, no watermark, no additional animals. Do not change his character design or make him a different breed. The exact 4x3 regular grid is critical for the game engine.
